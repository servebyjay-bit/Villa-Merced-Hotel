Our History

A regal portrait greets you at the lobby of Villa Merced Hotel — that of Maria Merced “Edith” Nakpil-Rabat, a beauty queen, public servant, and granddaughter of revolutionaries who carried a deep love for Mati.

Established in 2009 and thoughtfully renovated in October 2025, Villa Merced continues to honor its story while evolving with the times.
