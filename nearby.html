Nearby

Dining

Restaurants Nearby

Coi's Boneless Lechon

3-min walk

Bonifacio Street

Specializes in authentic Cebu-style boneless lechon, available in original and spicy variants. Located at Bonifacio Town Center near the Baywalk, offering lechon rice meals and packaged lechon for takeaway.

Ciangi

4-min walk

Quezon Street

A well-loved Mati classic serving comforting Filipino dishes and short-order options, with the bakareta as a standout specialty alongside well-regarded desserts.

Bador's Native Chicken

3-min walk

Rizal Street

A long-standing local favorite offering a budget-friendly experience where guests personally select their chicken, freshly grilled to order. Both branches are within walking distance.
